Open Sans Regular
=================

File:   OpenSans-Regular.ttf
Source: https://github.com/googlefonts/opensans (fonts/ttf/OpenSans-Regular.ttf)
License: Apache License 2.0
         https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The font is linked into the application image (see EMBED_FILES in
main/CMakeLists.txt) and read directly from flash by stb_truetype, no copy in
RAM is made.  To use another font, replace this file and keep the name, or
adjust EMBED_FILES and the symbol names in main/ttf.c.
